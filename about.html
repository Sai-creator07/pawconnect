About PawConnect

Connecting Paws with Loving Homes.

Our purpose

PawConnect is a pet adoption listing platform. It connects people who want to adopt a pet with people who need to find a pet a new home. One visitor can browse available animals, read their full profiles, and create a listing of their own.

Our mission

We want every adoption to start with clear, honest information. A good listing tells you who the pet is, what care it needs, and where it is, so the match lasts for the pet's whole life.

How we encourage responsible adoption

  • Every profile shows age, health information and personality in the same place.
  • Adopters are encouraged to ask about vaccinations, vet history and daily care before deciding.
  • Listers are asked to describe the kind of home that would suit their pet.
  • We remind adopters that a pet is a long-term commitment of time, money and love.
